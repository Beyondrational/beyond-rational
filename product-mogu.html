Mogu.

Soft, velvety acoustic panels grown from mycelium and upcycled textile residue. A 3D-shaped surface engineered to maximise absorption, brought to Denmark by Beyond Rational.

Beyond Rational · Copenhagen

The idea

Grown, not
machined.

01
Mycelium, not foam

Panels are grown from mycelium combined with upcycled textile residue — a soft, foam-like material that reaches its final shape through biological growth, not chemical processing.

02
Shaped for absorption

A velvety, three-dimensional surface is engineered specifically to absorb the frequencies of speech — tuned for offices, meeting rooms and homes alike.

03
Circular by design

VOC-free and 100% circular, with a screw-less fixing system for easy mounting and removal. Naturally white by default, with a wider colour range available.
