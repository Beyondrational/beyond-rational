Mogu.

Soft, velvety acoustic panels grown from mycelium and upcycled textile residue — nature-grown, engineered to perform. A 3D-shaped surface built to maximise absorption, brought to Denmark by Beyond Rational.

The growth

Cultivated,
not carved.

01
Mycelium, not foam

Panels are grown from mycelium combined with upcycled textile residue — a soft, foam-like material that reaches its final shape through biological growth, not chemical processing. Nature does the engineering.

02
Shaped for absorption

A velvety, three-dimensional surface, radically reshaped to absorb the frequencies of speech — tuned for offices, meeting rooms and homes alike.

03
Circular by design

VOC-free and 100% circular, with a screw-less fixing system for easy mounting and removal. Naturally white by default, with a wider colour range available.
